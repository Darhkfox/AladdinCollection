Aladdin Collection – PC Edition V1.0: all three classic versions of Disney's Aladdin – Mega Drive / Genesis (Virgin Games, 1993), Super Nintendo (Capcom, 1993) and Master System (SIMS, 1994) – in one launcher for Windows and Android, with true widescreen up to 21:9 and a load of modern extras.

⬇️ Download (free): https://github.com/Darhkfox/AladdinCollection/releases/latest
💻 Source code: https://github.com/Darhkfox/AladdinCollection

⚠️ You need your own ROMs – no game data is included. It needs these versions:
• Aladdin (Mega Drive) – Aladdin (U) [!].bin (USA)
• Aladdin (SNES) – Aladdin (USA).sfc
• Aladdin (Master System) – Disney's Aladdin (Europe).sms
On Windows, put them next to the exe or in a roms folder (zipped is fine; they're found by their contents, so file names don't matter). On Android, tap Add ROMs.

✨ Features
• One launcher for all three games, with live previews – keyboard, gamepad or mouse
• Two menu styles: Arabian Nights or Classic
• Widescreen 16:10, 16:9, 2:1 and 21:9 for all three games – more of each level really drawn at the sides, with enemies and items across the whole width; the HUD re-anchored to the edges (or not)
• Mega Drive: the Rug Ride, the title, story cards and Level Complete drawn wide; boss arenas framed
• SNES: every parallax layer and raster band moving as on the console; the lamp tower's gold walls; the staff roll wide
• Master System: wide story scenes, intro and title; characters walk in from the wide picture's edge
• CRT (4:3 TV) or square pixels, five filters, RetroArch slang shader presets (seven CRT ones included), colour profiles
• Level select for every level of all three games
• Cheats, including the developers' own: the Mega Drive debug level skip, map view and cheat mode, Capcom's debug mode on the SNES, the Master System's AIQY invincibility and TEST MODE
• 54 achievements, with an Achievements page and Export to CSV (Playnite's columns, with a badge for each one)
• Save states, rewind and fast forward
• Remove Slowdown for the SNES game
• High Frame Rate on 100 Hz+ displays, black frame insertion, V-Sync, integer scaling
• Music player & sound test for every tune and effect, with channel meters
• Bring your own CD-quality soundtracks: MD+ packs for the Mega Drive game, MSU-1 packs for the SNES game
• Your own covers and launcher background, Show Games, update notifications
• Xbox, PlayStation and other pads, every button rebindable
• Runs on its own from-scratch Mega Drive, SNES and Master System cores – nothing to install
